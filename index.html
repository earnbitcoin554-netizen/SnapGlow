How is Snap Score Calculated in 2026?

Your Snap Score is a composite algorithmic index. It increases with each snap sent and received, posting to your Story, and consistent streak activity. Snaps sent to multiple users simultaneously do not scale linearly, prioritizing organic 1-to-1 interactions.

Streak Hourglass Timer Rules

The hourglass indicator appears next to a friend’s name when roughly 4 to 7 hours remain in your 24-hour streak window. Both parties must send a photo or video snap (text chats do not qualify) to extinguish the timer and sustain the streak count.
